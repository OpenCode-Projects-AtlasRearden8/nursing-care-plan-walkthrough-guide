Education only · never medical advice · never patient data

Start with the map

A nursing care plan is just four questions written down:

1. What's going on?
assessment → cues → diagnosis
2. What should be different, by when?
SMART goals
3. What are we doing about it?
interventions
4. Did it work?
evaluation (and revise)

Each step below has a friendly markdown chapter in the docs/ folder.

The five chapters

  • 01 — Assessment Cues — noticing, clustering, and writing neutral specifics
  • 02 — NANDA-Style Language — problem + related to + as evidenced by
  • 03 — SMART Goals — goals a stranger could grade
  • 04 — Interventions — verb + action + frequency + method
  • 05 — Evaluation — met, partially met, unmet — honestly

Extras

  • Read Me First — ground rules and how the guide fits together
  • A Worked Example — one fictional plan, start to finish
  • Glossary & Cheat Sheet — definitions plus a pre-submission checklist
  • Blank Care-Plan Worksheet — fill in on screen or print it

Please read the small print

This site is educational material only. It is not medical advice, not a clinical decision tool, and not a documentation template for real patient records. Real patient questions belong with your instructor, preceptor, or care team. Please never enter real patient information anywhere on this site or in the worksheet — practice with fictional case studies from your program.

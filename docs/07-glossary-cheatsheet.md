# 07 - Glossary & Cheat Sheet

## Glossary (plain English)

- **Care plan** — the written record of: what's wrong from a nursing viewpoint, what should be different and by when, what nursing does about it, and whether it worked.
- **Assessment** — the systematic gathering of cues (things you observe/measure and things the person reports).
- **Cue** — a specific piece of data that's relevant, verifiable, and neutral.
- **Diagnosis (nursing)** — a NANDA-I-style label naming a response/condition nursing can treat, in the *problem + related to + as evidenced by* pattern.
- **Etiology ("related to")** — the cause-or-contributor you're aiming your interventions at; must be something nursing can influence.
- **Defining characteristics ("as evidenced by")** — the cues that *prove* the diagnosis.
- **Risk diagnosis** — a problem-no-yet diagnosis; written without "as evidenced by," with risk factors instead.
- **SMART goal** — specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound; about the *patient*, verb-first.
- **Independent intervention** — a nurse-initiated action (positioning, teaching, monitoring).
- **Collaborative/dependent intervention** — implementing orders or protocols with the care team.
- **Evaluation** — scoring the goal met / partially met / unmet at the specified time, citing the data, and deciding next steps.
- **Teach-back** — asking the patient to state/demonstrate what was taught, in their own words.
- **PES** — problem, etiology, signs/symptoms: the shorthand for the full diagnosis sentence.

## One-page cheat sheet

```
HOUSE-LIKE STRUCTURE OF A CARE PLAN
─────────────────────────────────────────
ASSESSMENT:  clustered, specific, neutral cues
    ↓
DIAGNOSIS:   [Problem] related to [nursing-actionable cause]
             as evidenced by [2–4 cues]
    ↓
GOAL:        Patient will [verb + behavior] [condition]
             by [time/date].        ← measurable? patient-centered?
    ↓
INTERVENTIONS: verb + action + frequency + method
             (each traces to etiology or goal)
    ↓
EVALUATION:  [met / partially met / unmet] + evidence + next step
             (feeds back into assessment → revise)
```

## Quality self-check before you submit

**Diagnosis**
- [ ] Label wording matches your approved NANDA-I reference
- [ ] Etiology is nursing-actionable, not a medical diagnosis
- [ ] "As evidenced by" lists 2–4 real cues from your assessment (n/a for risk dx)

**Goals**
- [ ] Every goal begins "Patient will…"
- [ ] Measurable — a stranger could score met/not met
- [ ] Time-bound with a real time or date
- [ ] At least one short-term and one long-term (if your template wants both)

**Interventions**
- [ ] Each one: verb + action + frequency + method
- [ ] Each traces to the etiology or the goal
- [ ] Independent and collaborative both represented, per your template
- [ ] Includes monitoring/what-to-escalate ("watch for") items

**Evaluation**
- [ ] Evaluated at the stated time, with the stated measure
- [ ] Status (met / partially met / unmet) explicitly stated
- [ ] Supporting data cited
- [ ] Next step named (revise, continue, new goal)

**Professional hygiene**
- [ ] No real patient identifiers anywhere (fake case study only)
- [ ] Neutral, non-judgmental language throughout
- [ ] Your program's template/format followed

## Disclaimer

Educational material only — not medical advice, not a clinical decision tool, not a documentation template for real patient records. NANDA-I labels evolve with each edition; always verify exact wording against your program's current approved reference and follow your facility's policies.

# 00 - Read Me First

## What this is

A friend-style walkthrough of how nursing students write care plans. Five skills, one worked example, one blank worksheet. That's the whole product.

## What a care plan actually is

Strip away the paperwork dread and a care plan is just four questions written down:

1. **What's going on?** (assessment → cues → diagnosis)
2. **What should be different by when?** (goals / outcomes)
3. **What are we doing about it?** (interventions)
4. **Did it work?** (evaluation)

Instructors care about the writing because the writing proves you can *think* like a nurse: notice things, name them, plan, and check yourself. It's a thinking exercise that happens to be on paper.

## The ground rules (non-negotiable)

- **Education only.** Nothing here is medical advice. Real patient questions go to your instructor, preceptor, or the care team.
- **No real patient data, ever.** Practice on case studies and simulations. If you write about a real person, remove anything that could identify them — name, room number, dates, employer, whatever your program's policy says. This website asks for none of it and stores none of it.
- **NANDA-I terminology changes with editions.** This guide teaches the *pattern* (problem → related to → as evidenced by). Confirm the exact current wording from your program's approved NANDA nursing diagnoses reference, because that's the source of truth your instructor grades against.
- **Your facility's policies beat any guide.** Including this one.

## How the parts fit together

```
Cues you notice ──► Diagnosis you name ──► Goal you aim for
                                              │
Evaluation ◄──────── Interventions you do ◄───┘
(the loop closes; revise as needed)
```

Every chapter builds on the one before it. A weak diagnosis makes the goal unmeasurable, which makes evaluation a shrug. Fix it at the first step and everything downstream gets easier.

## How to use this guide

1. Read chapters 01–05 in order (short chapters, plain language).
2. Read the worked example in 06.
3. Open the blank worksheet (site/index.html) and draft your own plan for a case study from your program — no real patient data.
4. Check yourself against the cheat sheet.

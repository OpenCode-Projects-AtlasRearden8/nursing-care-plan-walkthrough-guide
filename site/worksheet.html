Education only. This worksheet is a study aid, not medical advice and not a charting template. Nothing is saved or transmitted — closing the tab clears it.

Step 0 — Case study information

Fictional scenario only. No names of real people, no room numbers, no real dates.

Step 1 — Assessment cues

Neutral, specific, verifiable. Subjective and objective. Then cluster related ones.

Step 2 — Nursing diagnosis (PES)

Pattern: [problem] related to [nursing-actionable cause] as evidenced by [cues]. Verify exact label wording in your program's approved NANDA-I reference.

Step 3 — SMART goals

Start with “Patient will…” — measurable verb, condition, deadline. A stranger should be able to grade it.

Step 4 — Interventions

Verb + action + frequency + method. Each one traces to the etiology or the goal. Mark independent vs. collaborative.

Step 5 — Evaluation

At the stated time, with the stated measure: met / partially met / unmet + the evidence + what's next.
